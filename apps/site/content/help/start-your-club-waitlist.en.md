## Where things stand

Luvallplay isn't open for new clubs yet. One club runs on it today, a badminton club in Christchurch. We're building the tools that let any club set itself up, and we want to get them right before we hand them over.

So for now, "Start your club" means "join the waitlist". It takes about a minute.

## How to join

1. Go to [for clubs](/for-clubs) and choose **Start your club**.
2. Fill in the short form: your email, your name, your club's name, your sport, your city, and whether you're an organiser or a player.
3. Tick the box to say we can email you about it.
4. Check your inbox and tap the link to confirm your email.

Step 4 matters. We use what's called double opt-in: nobody is added to the list until they confirm from their own inbox. It stops typos and stops other people signing you up.

If the email hasn't arrived after a few minutes, look in your spam or junk folder. Still nothing? Fill in the form again and check the address for typos. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 30 days.

## What happens after you sign up

- Once you've confirmed your email, you're on the list.
- We'll send you updates on our progress. Not many, and only the kind you asked for.
- When clubs can be set up, we'll email you an invitation.

We haven't set a date, and we won't guess one here. We'd rather be late than hand you something half-finished.

Every email has a one-click "remove my details" link. Use it any time and you're off the list.

## What "founding club" means

The first clubs we bring on are our founding clubs. Luvallplay is free for founding clubs. Paid plans come later, and clubs get notice before anything changes. Players are free.

We'll also ask founding clubs what works and what doesn't. Telling us is the most useful thing you can do, and it shapes what we build next.

## Would you rather talk it through?

Choose **Book a walkthrough** on the [for clubs](/for-clubs) page. Tell us a little about your club and we'll get in touch to arrange a time. Bring your questions and your awkward cases. We like those.

## What setting up is planned to look like

When clubs can sign up, you'll be able to set up your club in a few short steps. The plan is for it to take about 5 minutes:

1. Sign in with a code sent to your email.
2. Tell us about your club: name, country, sports, where you play and your languages.
3. Pick your colours. See [make it your club](/help/make-it-your-club-colours-and-brand).
4. Say how you charge, if you charge at all. See [choose how your club charges](/help/choose-how-your-club-charges).
5. Pick the tools you want. See [modules: pick what you need](/help/modules-pick-what-you-need).
6. Invite your co-organisers and bring in your member list.
7. Launch, and share your club's link with your players.

This is the plan, and details may change before it opens. You'll be able to change every choice later.
