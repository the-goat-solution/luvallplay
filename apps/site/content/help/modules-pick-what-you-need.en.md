## Small by default

A volunteer running a social night doesn't need an accounts department. So Luvallplay is planned around a small core that every club gets, plus optional extras called modules. You switch on the ones you want and never see the rest.

This is planned for when clubs can sign up. It isn't available to new clubs today, and the list may change before then.

## The core: always included

This is what a free, no-money club needs to run its sessions. It's always on.

- **Home.** What needs your attention: your next session and its sign-ups, people asking to join, unread messages.
- **Play days.** Your regular schedule and one-off days, the sign-up list and waitlist, opening and closing sign-ups, cancelling a day and ticking off attendance.
- **People.** Your members, requests to join, invitations, and switching a player on or off. Your club decides who plays.
- **Messages.** An inbox for messages from members, and announcements to everyone.
- **Website.** A simple club page: who you are, when and where you play, how to join.
- **Settings.** Club details, languages, your look, how you charge, your modules, who your organisers are, and your club's data.

For a free club with nothing else switched on, that's the whole menu.

## Optional modules

### For running sessions

- **Casual play.** Let visitors book a single session.
- **Court board.** A screen for organisers to rotate players on and off the courts or fields during a session.
- **Round robin.** Draws and scores for a mini tournament.
- **Multiple venues.** For clubs that play in more than one place.

### For money

These only appear if your club charges. See [choose how your club charges](/help/choose-how-your-club-charges).

- **Memberships.** Plans, renewals and a membership list.
- **Payments to check.** Unpaid bookings and memberships, with a button to mark them as paid.
- **Finance and reports.** Income, expenses and a report for your annual meeting.
- **Shop.** Club shirts and gear.
- **Donations.** A "support the club" option.

### For your players

- **Arena.** Challenges and a club ladder. See [challenges and the ladder](/help/challenges-and-the-ladder).
- **Gallery.** Photos from your sessions, checked by an organiser before they appear.
- **Vision.** Game analysis from a phone video. Badminton only, and for now it is only open to members of the first club on Luvallplay. See [Vision](/vision).
- **Juniors.** A junior roster with guardian contacts. See [juniors and guardians](/help/juniors-and-guardians).

## Presets: a quick start

Don't want to choose one by one? Setup is planned to offer 4 starting points:

- **Free social club.** The core only.
- **Social club.** The core, plus court board and casual play.
- **Competitive club.** All of that, plus round robin, Arena and gallery.
- **Coaching academy.** All of that, plus memberships and requests for extra sessions.

Pick the closest one, then add or remove modules to suit.

## How switching works

- **Off means hidden, not deleted.** Turn a module off and its screens disappear. Its information is kept, and comes back when you turn it on again.
- **Owners decide.** Club owners will be able to change modules at any time in settings. Other organisers can see what's on.
- **Some modules need others.** A shop needs a way to be paid, for example. The switch tells you why if something can't be turned on yet.
- **You get a warning.** If you turn off something members are using, such as Arena with challenges in progress, you'll see what's affected first.

## How to choose

1. Write down what your club does in a normal week. Only that.
2. Start with the smallest preset that covers it.
3. Run 2 or 3 sessions.
4. Add one module at a time, when someone actually asks for it.

A simple club that works beats a clever one nobody understands.
