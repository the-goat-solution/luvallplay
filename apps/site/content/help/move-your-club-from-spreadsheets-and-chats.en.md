## Why bother before you can sign up?

Most clubs run on a group chat, a spreadsheet and one person's memory. It works, until that person goes on holiday.

Luvallplay isn't open for new clubs yet, and the import tools described here are planned, not live. But the tidying-up is useful today, whatever you end up using. Do it now and moving day is short.

## What to gather

### Your member list

One row per person, in a spreadsheet you can save as a CSV file. The plan is that you'll be able to import members from a CSV file, or paste them straight from a spreadsheet.

Useful columns:

- First name
- Last name
- Email address (this is how members will sign in, so check it's current)
- Mobile number, if you keep it
- Membership type and the date it runs to, if your club has memberships

Tidy as you go. Remove people who left years ago. Merge duplicates. Fix the email addresses that bounce.

### Your sessions

- The days and times you play
- The venue and its address
- How many courts or fields you have, and how many players that fits
- Whether casual players can book a single session
- Any dates you already know you won't play, such as holidays or hall closures

### Your fees

- Whether you charge at all. Plenty of clubs don't.
- The price of a casual session
- Your membership plans and prices
- How people pay you today: cash, bank transfer or card

Read [choose how your club charges](/help/choose-how-your-club-charges) to see which setup will suit you.

### Your look

- Your logo. An SVG file is best. A PNG or JPG at least 512 pixels wide is fine.
- Your club colours, if you have them
- A few sentences about your club: who you are, what level you play, who's welcome

No logo? No problem. You'll be able to pick from ready-made colour schemes. See [make it your club](/help/make-it-your-club-colours-and-brand).

### Your people

- Who else helps run the club, with their email addresses, so you can invite them as co-organisers
- Who should hear from new players, and at which email address

## Tell your members first

Your member list is personal information. Before you move it anywhere:

- Tell your members what you're doing and why. A short message in the group chat is enough.
- Only bring across people who are actually members now.
- Only bring what you need. Names and emails, yes. Old notes about people, no.
- If you have junior members, read [juniors and guardians](/help/juniors-and-guardians) first. Juniors are planned to be managed by a guardian and won't have their own sign-in.

## Plan the switch

A few things that make a move go smoothly for any club:

1. **Pick a start date.** The beginning of a month or a term works well.
2. **Run both for 2 weeks.** Keep the chat going, but post the sign-up link there for every session.
3. **Help in person.** Stand by the door at a session and help people sign in on their phones. It takes a minute each.
4. **Then stop taking names in the chat.** Be kind, and be firm. One list is the whole point.
5. **Keep the chat for chat.** It's still the best place for banter and photos.

## What happens to the old spreadsheet?

Keep a copy somewhere safe until you're sure everything came across. Then delete the extra copies, so members' details aren't sitting in old files.
