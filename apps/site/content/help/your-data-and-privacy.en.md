## The short version

We collect as little as we can, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you. If you want your details gone, tell us and they're gone.

This article is a summary. The full notice is on the [privacy page](/privacy), and that's the one that counts.

## This covers the website only

This article is about the Luvallplay website. Club apps and club websites have their own notices. Separate terms and a privacy policy for the Luvallplay apps will be published before clubs go live.

If you're a member of a club, your club decides what it asks you for and why. Ask your club's organisers if you want to know what they hold about you.

## What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site. The forms are: start your club, book a walkthrough, invite a club, get the app, and contact.

Depending on the form, that can be:

- Your email address
- Your name
- Your club's name, sport and city
- Whether you're an organiser or a player
- Your message

We also keep 3 things about the form itself: the consent wording you agreed to and when, whether you confirmed your email, and which language you used.

## What we don't do

- No advertising
- No tracking cookies
- No following you across other websites
- No selling or renting your details
- No building a profile of you

## Cookies and your browser

- **One cookie.** It's called "lang" and it remembers whether you chose English or Vietnamese. It lasts 1 year.
- **Your theme.** If you choose light or dark mode, your browser remembers that itself. It isn't sent to us.

That's why there's no cookie banner. There's nothing to ask your permission for.

## How we count visits

We use Vercel Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies. It counts page views in total, so we can see which pages are useful. It doesn't build a profile of you.

## How we stop spam

- For up to 24 hours we keep a scrambled, one-way fingerprint of the internet address a form was sent from. It lets us limit how many forms one source can send. We don't keep the address itself.
- Each form has a hidden field that people never see and robots tend to fill in.
- If it's switched on, a Cloudflare check confirms you're human.

## Why we use your details

- To reply to you
- To send the updates you asked for
- To invite you when clubs can be set up

Nothing else.

## How long we keep them

- **You never confirmed your email:** deleted after 30 days.
- **You confirmed:** kept until you ask us to remove them, or 24 months after we last contacted you, whichever comes first.

## Getting your details removed

Two ways, both quick:

1. Use the one-click "remove my details" link at the bottom of any email from us.
2. [Contact us](/contact) and ask.

You can also ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. We answer within 20 working days.

## Who helps us run the site

A few companies process information for us: Vercel hosts the site, Neon runs the database in Sydney, Australia, and Resend sends our emails. Sanity holds the words on our pages, but nothing about visitors. Cloudflare runs the human check, if it's on.

## Under 16?

Ask a parent or guardian before you use the forms on this site. For how junior players are planned to work in clubs, see [juniors and guardians](/help/juniors-and-guardians).

## Your club's data

Our intent is simple: your club's data is your club's. When clubs can sign up, organisers are planned to be able to export their club's data and handle members' requests to delete theirs. The details will be in the terms we publish before clubs go live.

## Not happy with how we handled something?

Tell us first, so we can fix it: [contact us](/contact). The [privacy page](/privacy) also explains how to complain to New Zealand's Privacy Commissioner.
