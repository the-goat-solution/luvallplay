## Where the finder is today

The [club finder](/find-a-club) is a short, hand-picked list for now. We only show clubs we know are real and running, and today that means one: Three Wolves Badminton Club in Christchurch, New Zealand.

A wider finder, with more clubs and more sports, is coming soon. We'd rather show you one real club than a map full of guesses.

## If you're in Christchurch

Three Wolves plays badminton twice a week, on Wednesday and Friday evenings. Visitors are welcome to book a casual session, which is a single visit with no membership needed.

### How to book a casual session

1. Go to [threewolvesbadminton.org](https://threewolvesbadminton.org).
2. Find the upcoming sessions and choose **Book a casual spot**.
3. Pick the session you want. You'll see how many spots are left.
4. Enter your name, email address and phone number.
5. Pay by card on the secure payment step. The price is shown before you pay.
6. Check your inbox. Your booking confirmation arrives by email.

That's it. Turn up and play.

### If the session is full

Choose **Join the waitlist** instead. It costs nothing to join. If a spot opens, the club emails you a link to book it. There's more in [bookings, waitlists and can't make it](/help/bookings-waitlists-and-cant-make-it).

### Before you go

- Check the club's site for the venue and start time.
- Wear comfortable sports clothes and clean indoor court shoes.
- Bring a water bottle.
- Ask the club about equipment if you don't have your own.
- Arrive 10 minutes early and say it's your first time. Someone will show you how the night runs.

### Liked it?

You can keep booking casual sessions, or ask about joining as a member. The club's site explains the options.

## Trying any club for the first time

Wherever you play, a first visit is easier with a little preparation:

- **Message ahead.** Ask what level the session suits and whether you need to book.
- **Be honest about your level.** "I played at school" and "I play twice a week" are both fine answers. They get you into the right games.
- **Ask how rotation works.** Every club does it differently. Nobody minds the question.
- **Play with everyone.** Social sessions mix partners and opponents. That's how you meet people.
- **Give it 2 visits.** One night is a small sample. The second is when it starts to feel familiar.

## What it costs

Luvallplay is free for players. A club may charge for its own sessions or memberships, and that money goes to the club. You'll always see the club's price before you pay.
