## Why sign-ups matter

A hall has only so many courts. A field fits only so many teams. When your club knows who's coming, everybody gets more games and less standing around.

This article describes how it works today for members of the first club on Luvallplay. Clubs that join Luvallplay later will get the same tools, though each club sets its own rules.

## Two ways to play

- **As a member.** Your membership includes your regular day each week. You don't book it. Your spot is held for you.
- **As a casual player.** You book single sessions, one at a time. Members can book a casual spot on other days too.

## Booking a casual session

1. Open your club's site and find the upcoming play days.
2. Pick a session. You'll see how many spots are left.
3. Enter your details, or sign in if you have an account.
4. Pay on the secure payment step. The price is shown first.
5. Look for the confirmation email.

Once you're signed in, your play calendar shows your sessions. Each one has a label, such as **Included** for your membership day or **Booked** for a casual spot.

Some sessions open for booking closer to the day. If a session says it opens later, come back then.

## When the session is full

Full doesn't mean no. Choose **Join the waitlist**.

- Joining is free. You don't pay anything to wait.
- If a spot opens, you get an email with a link to book it.
- The link is how you claim the spot. Being on the waitlist isn't a booking on its own.
- Spots can open late, even on the day. Keep an eye on your inbox.

If you're on the waitlist and your plans change, there's nothing to cancel. Just don't use the link.

## "I can't make it"

Life happens. Work runs late, the car won't start, your knee complains.

If you're a member and you can't come on your regular day:

1. Sign in and open your play calendar.
2. Find the day and choose **Can't make it**.

That's all. Your spot is freed for someone else that day, which often means a person on the waitlist gets to play.

Changed your mind? Choose **I can come after all** on the same day.

### Why it's worth the tap

- Someone else gets a game.
- Your organisers can plan with real numbers.
- Nobody stands around wondering whether to wait for you.

Do it as early as you can. An hour's notice helps. A day's notice helps more.

## Can't make a casual booking?

Each club sets its own rules for changing or refunding a casual booking. Check your club's site, or message the organisers as soon as you know. Even when a refund isn't possible, telling them means the spot doesn't go to waste.

## Good booking manners

- **Book early.** Popular sessions fill up.
- **Book for yourself.** If you're bringing a friend, they need their own spot.
- **Turn up on time.** The first games get made in the first 10 minutes.
- **Tell someone if you're late.** A quick message keeps your place in the rotation.
- **Don't book "just in case".** A spot you might not use is a spot someone else can't have.

## Something looks wrong

- **No confirmation email?** Check spam or junk, then check you typed your email correctly.
- **Booked the wrong day?** Message your organisers straight away.
- **Can't sign in?** See [signing in with an email code](/help/signing-in-with-an-email-code).
- **Paid twice by mistake?** Contact your club. Payments go to the club, so the club handles refunds.
