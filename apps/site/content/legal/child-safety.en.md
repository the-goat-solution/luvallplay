This page sets out Luvallplay's standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation. They apply to everyone who uses the Luvallplay app, in every club and in every country.

## Our standards

- No child sexual abuse material, and nothing that sexualises a child. That covers photos, videos, drawings, text and links.
- No grooming, no sexual approach to a child, no threats or blackmail over images, and no asking for, offering or trading this kind of material.
- No content or behaviour that puts a child at risk of sexual abuse or exploitation.
- There are no exceptions and no warnings. An account that breaks these standards is suspended in every club, and stays suspended if the person deletes the account and signs up again.

## Who the social features are for

- The social features (a profile, posts, comments and photos shared between players) are being switched on club by club. Wherever they are on, they are for adults only. Before you can join, you confirm that you are 18 or older and accept the community rules.
- Children under 16 don't have their own sign-in. A parent or guardian manages a junior's profile, and a junior profile can't join the social features, post, comment or follow anyone.
- A photo is checked before it can appear in a post.
- The social features have no private messages.

## How to report

- In the app, every post, comment and profile has a menu with **Report**. Choose "Child safety". You can also block the person.
- You don't need an account to tell us. Email {{safetyEmail}} or use the [contact page](/contact).
- Please don't save, forward or repost the material, even to report it. Tell us where you saw it and we'll find it.
- If a child is in danger right now, call the police first: 111 in New Zealand, 113 in Vietnam. Vietnam's child protection line is 111.

## What we do with a report

1. A child-safety report goes to Luvallplay's own moderators, never to the club. We look at it within 12 hours.
2. We suspend the account and take the content down while we look.
3. We keep a sealed copy of what was reported, so it can be handed to the authorities. It stays even if the account is deleted.
4. We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities responsible for it in the country concerned.
5. We never tell the reported person who made the report.
