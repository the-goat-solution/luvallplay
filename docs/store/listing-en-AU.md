## Full description (max 4000)
```
Luvallplay is the app for people who play club sport. One app, every club you belong to.

Book and manage your play
• See the next session at your club and book your spot in a few taps
• Can't make it? Cancel in the app and the club knows straight away
• Check your membership, what's included and when it renews
• Pay your club's session fees and memberships securely online

Compete with your club
• Challenge other players and confirm match results
• Climb the club leaderboard and see how you rank in the Arena
• Earn points and rank-ups as you play

Track your matches with the Vision tracker
• Point your phone at the court, or analyse a match video, and get hits, rallies, speed and distance for each player
• The analysis runs on your phone: your video stays with you

Stay in touch
• Your club's messages and announcements in one inbox
• Choose which notifications you want: challenges, match results, play-day reminders and more. Nothing is switched on until you say so
• Share your photos and see your club's gallery

Your data, your control
• Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email: no password to remember
• Download a copy of your data, or delete your account, from Settings
• English and Vietnamese

Luvallplay is made for clubs of every size. Ask your club to join, or start your own at luvallplay.com.

Players aged 18 and over. Juniors are managed by a parent or guardian account (coming later).
```
