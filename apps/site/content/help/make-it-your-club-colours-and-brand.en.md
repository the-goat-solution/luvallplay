## Your club, not ours

Members should open the app and see their club. Their colours, their logo, their name at the top.

When you set up your club, you'll be able to choose how it looks in one of 2 ways: pick a ready-made colour scheme, or bring your own brand. Both are planned for when clubs can sign up. They aren't available to new clubs today, and details may change before then.

## Option 1: pick a colour scheme

There will be 12 schemes to choose from. Each has a main colour for headers and buttons, and an accent colour for highlights and live moments.

- Court Green
- Midnight Rally
- Shuttle Mono
- Sunset Smash
- Ocean Drive
- Royal Drop
- Cherry Net
- Electric Serve
- Jungle Rally
- Aurora
- Plum Clear
- Arctic Smash

Court Green is the default, and it's Luvallplay's own.

You'll see each scheme on your own club's name, a session card and a button before you choose. One tap and it's done.

Every scheme has a light and a dark version, and every one is tested so text stays easy to read in both. You can't pick a bad one.

## Option 2: bring your own brand

Already have a logo and colours your members know? Use them.

### Logo

- Upload an SVG, PNG or JPG. PNG and JPG files need to be at least 512 pixels wide.
- You can add a second version for dark backgrounds.
- We'll make the small versions for you, such as the browser icon, the round club picture and the email header. You check a preview of each.

### Fonts

- Choose from a list of fonts we've checked, or upload your club's own.
- If you upload a font, you'll confirm your club has the licence to use it on the web.
- We'll check the font covers both English and Vietnamese letters, and warn you if anything is missing.

### Colours

- Type in your colour codes, or pick them by eye.
- Or let us suggest colours from your logo.
- Or point us at your club's existing website and we'll read the colours from there.

Then you choose which is your main colour and which is your accent.

## What happens if a colour is hard to read?

Some lovely colours make terrible backgrounds for text. Pale yellow behind white writing, for example.

If one of your colours can't carry readable text, we keep your colour for your logo and decoration, and adjust the shade used behind text. You'll see the original and the adjusted version side by side, with a one-sentence reason. A dark-mode version is made the same way.

This isn't us being fussy. It means every member can read your club's pages, including people with low vision and anyone squinting at a phone in a bright hall.

## Where your look shows up

- Your club's website
- Your club's pages inside the app
- Emails to your members
- The QR poster for your venue

The sign-in screens and the app icon stay Luvallplay, so players always know where they are.

## Tips for choosing

- **Start with what's on your shirts.** If your club has a kit, match it.
- **Check it in both light and dark.** Many players use dark mode at night.
- **Look at it on a phone.** That's where most members will see it.
- **Ask 2 or 3 members.** A quick opinion now saves a debate later.
- **Don't agonise.** You'll be able to change your scheme, or switch back to a ready-made one, at any time. Changes show as a preview before they go live.
