## What we collect

We only collect what you type into one of the forms on this site:

- Start your club (the waitlist)
- Book a walkthrough
- Invite a club
- Get the app: notify me
- Contact

Depending on the form, that can include:

- Your email address
- Your name
- Your club's name
- Your sport
- Your city
- Your role (organiser or player)
- Your message

With each form we also record:

- The wording of the consent you agreed to, and when you agreed
- Whether you confirmed your email address (we use double opt-in, so we ask you to confirm from your own inbox)
- The language you were using

If you tell us about another person or another club, for example through the invite form, please only share details you're entitled to share.

## How we stop spam

- **Rate limiting.** To limit how many forms one source can send, we keep a salted one-way hash of the IP address for up to 24 hours. We don't keep the IP address itself, and the hash can't be turned back into it.
- **A hidden field.** Each form has a hidden anti-spam field that people don't see and automated senders tend to fill in.
- **A human check.** If it's enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're human before a form is sent.

## Cookies and browser storage

- We use no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies.
- We use one functional cookie, named "lang". It remembers your language choice and lasts 1 year.
- Your light or dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device.

## Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics. It's cookieless. It gives us aggregated page-view counts, so we can see which pages are useful. It doesn't build a personal profile of you, and it doesn't track you across other websites.

## Why we use your information

- To reply to you
- To send you the updates you asked for
- To invite you when clubs can be set up

We don't use it for anything else. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

## Who processes information for us

These companies process information on our behalf, so we can run the website:

- **Vercel:** hosting and analytics
- **Neon:** the database. It's stored in Sydney, Australia.
- **Resend:** sending email
- **Sanity:** page content only. It holds no visitor data.
- **Cloudflare:** Turnstile, only if it's enabled

Some of these companies are based outside New Zealand, so your information may be processed in other countries. We don't share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

- **Unconfirmed sign-ups** are deleted after 30 days.
- **Confirmed details** are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until 24 months after our last contact with you, whichever comes first.
- **The hashed IP address** used for rate limiting is kept for up to 24 hours.
