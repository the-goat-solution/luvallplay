## No password needed

You never set a password. When you sign in, we send a 6-digit code to your email. Type it in and you're done.

Why? Passwords get forgotten, reused and stolen. A fresh code each time is simpler for you and safer for your club.

This article describes how sign-in works today for members of the first club on Luvallplay. Other clubs will sign in the same way when they join.

## How to sign in

1. Open your club's site and choose to sign in.
2. Enter your email address and choose **Email me a code**.
3. Open your email. Look for a message with a 6-digit code.
4. Type the code in and choose **Sign in**.

The email also has a sign-in link. Tapping it signs you in without typing the code. Use whichever is easier.

The first time, you'll be asked for your name so the club knows who you are.

## Good to know

- **A code lasts 10 minutes.** After that, ask for a new one.
- **You get 5 tries per code.** After that, ask for a new one.
- **Only the newest code counts.** If you asked twice, use the code from the latest email.
- **You stay signed in for 90 days** on that device, so you won't do this often.
- **Use the same email every time.** Your bookings and membership are tied to it.

## Signing in with a text instead

At the first club you can sometimes choose **Text me a code** and get the code by text message. It works for New Zealand mobile numbers only, and it isn't always available. If you don't see the option, or it isn't working, use your email. Email always works.

## The code hasn't arrived

Try these in order:

1. **Wait a minute.** Email is usually quick, but not always instant.
2. **Check spam or junk.** In Gmail, also look under Promotions and Updates.
3. **Check the address.** Go back and look for a typo. A missing letter is the usual culprit.
4. **Check you're in the right inbox.** If you have work and personal email, the code went to the one you typed.
5. **Ask for a new code.** Choose **Send a new code**, then use the newest email.
6. **Mark it as safe.** If the email landed in spam, mark it "not spam" so the next one arrives properly.

## Messages you might see

- **"That code isn't right."** Check each digit and try again. Make sure you're using the latest email.
- **"That code has expired."** More than 10 minutes have passed. Ask for a new one.
- **"Too many tries."** Wait a few minutes, then ask for a new code.
- **A security check appears.** Complete it and carry on. It keeps robots out.

## Using a work or school email?

Some organisations filter or delay automatic emails. If codes never reach you there, use a personal email address instead. If you're already a member under the work address, ask your club to update your email first, so you don't end up with 2 accounts.

## Changed your email address?

Your email is linked to your bookings and payments, so you can't change it yourself. Send your club a message, from the app if you can still sign in, or in person at a session. They can check it's you and update it.

## Keeping your account safe

- **Never share a code.** Nobody from your club or from Luvallplay will ever ask for it.
- **Didn't ask for a code?** Ignore the email. Without the code, nobody can sign in.
- **Sign out on shared devices.** On a borrowed phone or a library computer, sign out when you finish.
- **Look after your inbox.** Your email is the key to your account, so give it a strong password and turn on its extra security if it has any.

## Still stuck?

Tell your club's organisers at the next session, or [contact us](/contact) with the email address you're trying to use. Don't send us any codes.
