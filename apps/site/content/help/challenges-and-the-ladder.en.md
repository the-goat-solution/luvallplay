## A reason to play one more game

Social play is great. Sometimes you want a game that counts.

Arena is where that happens. You pair up, challenge another pair, play, and both sides confirm the score. Wins earn points, points move you up your club's ranks, and the leaderboard shows where everyone stands.

Arena is live today at the first club on Luvallplay, a badminton club, so this article describes badminton doubles. Ladders are kept per club and per sport. Rankings for other sports are coming soon.

## How a challenge works

### 1. Pair up

Challenges are played in pairs. Sign in, open Arena and team up with a partner. Show them your code or QR code, type in theirs, or send them an invite link. You can name your pair if you like.

You can split up between games and pair with someone else. Your record together stays.

### 2. Throw down or take one on

- **Throw down.** Put your pair up for a challenge. Choose how long it stays open: today, 3 days or 7 days. Add a note if you want.
- **Take one on.** Look through the open challenges and accept one.

### 3. Sort out when and where

Once a challenge is accepted, the 2 pairs agree a time and a court. Most people play it at a normal club session.

### 4. Check in

When you meet, one pair reads out a short code and the other types it in. That starts the game, and it proves all 4 of you were actually there.

### 5. Play

One game to 21. You need to win by 2, and 30 caps it.

### 6. Enter and confirm the score

One pair enters the final score. The other pair confirms it. If nobody answers, the score confirms itself after 24 hours.

If the score looks wrong, say so. It goes to the club's admins, who sort it out.

## Points and ranks

- **Wins earn points.** Each club can give its points its own name. You also earn them for turning up to play days and for round robin wins.
- **Losses never cost points.** So there's no reason to dodge a hard game.
- **Some wins earn a bonus.** Beating a higher-ranked pair, playing a pair you haven't faced before and winning several in a row all earn extra.
- **Points move you up the ranks.** The ladder runs through named ranks from the bottom to the top, and each club can name its own.
- **The top ranks need looking after.** Stop playing for 60 days and they start to tarnish. Play again to polish them back up.

There's also a crown. One pair holds it, and beating them earns a bonus.

To keep things fair, there's a limit on how often the same 4 players can play each other for points.

## The leaderboard

The leaderboard lives in Arena. It shows your club's players and where they stand. It's your club's own ladder, not a national ranking. You appear under your display name, which you can set in your account.

## Play it in the right spirit

- **Call your own lines honestly.** There's no umpire. Your word is the system.
- **If in doubt, replay the point.** It's quicker than arguing.
- **Enter the score together,** at the court, while you both remember it.
- **Challenge up.** You have nothing to lose and something to learn.
- **Accept challenges from newer pairs.** Somebody did it for you once.
- **Shake hands.** Win or lose, you'll see them next week.

## Common questions

**Do I have to take part?** No. Arena is optional. Plenty of members just play socially.

**Can I play singles?** For now, Arena is for pairs.

**We couldn't finish the game.** You can record that a pair retired instead of entering a score.

**Can I see stats from my game?** If someone filmed the challenge with Vision, yes. See [Vision: film your game](/help/vision-film-your-game).

**Will my club have this?** When clubs can sign up, Arena is planned as an optional module each club can switch on. See [modules: pick what you need](/help/modules-pick-what-you-need).
