## Three ways to run the money

Every club handles money differently. Some never charge a cent. Some collect cash at the door. Some take cards.

When you set up your club, you'll be able to choose one of 3 payment modes. This is planned for when clubs can sign up, and isn't available to new clubs today. The details below are for clubs in New Zealand and Australia. We're still working out the right setup for clubs in Vietnam.

### Free play

Your club doesn't charge players. That's a full, proper choice, not a workaround.

- Players sign up for sessions for free. You still get capacity limits, waitlists, "I can't make it" and attendance.
- Joining the club is free too.
- No prices, no checkout and no "payment pending" anywhere. Your players never see a price tag.

### Cash or bank transfer

Your club charges, and players pay you directly.

- Players see the price, your bank details and a payment reference to copy. If you allow it, they can also pay cash at the venue.
- A booking shows as awaiting payment until an organiser marks it as paid.
- The money goes straight into your club's bank account, or your cash tin. It never passes through Luvallplay.

### Online payments

Players pay by card when they book.

- Card payments run through your club's own Stripe account, connected to Luvallplay.
- You can keep cash and bank transfer alongside cards if you want.
- The money goes straight to your club's Stripe account.

## Why online payments need your own Stripe account

Luvallplay never holds your club's money, and one club never collects money for another. That's deliberate. It keeps your money yours, and it keeps everyone on the right side of the rules for handling other people's money.

So card payments only work when the club itself can receive them. That takes 2 things:

- **Your club is incorporated.** For example an incorporated society, a charitable trust or a company. An informal group of friends usually can't open a payment account in the club's name.
- **Your club has its own Stripe account.** Stripe is the payment company that processes the cards.

If both are true, you'll be able to connect your Stripe account during setup. If either isn't, you'll be set up for cash and bank transfer, which works well and is how plenty of clubs run.

## The 3 questions setup will ask

1. Do you charge players to play? If no, you're a free-play club. Done.
2. Is your club incorporated? If no, or you're not sure, you're set up for cash and bank transfer.
3. Does your club have its own Stripe account? If yes, connect it and turn on online payments. If no, cash and bank transfer it is.

## You can change your mind later

- **Free to paid.** New prices only apply to bookings and memberships made after you switch.
- **Moving up to cards.** When your club incorporates and opens a Stripe account, you'll be able to turn on card payments. Your prices and history carry over.
- **Mixed is fine.** A club that charges will still be able to make a single session free, such as an open night.

Players get a notice when the way they pay changes.

## What does Luvallplay cost on top?

Players are free. Clubs are free for founding clubs. Paid plans come later, and clubs get notice before anything changes. See [pricing](/pricing).

Stripe charges its own processing fees on card payments. Those are between your club and Stripe, and Stripe publishes them on its website.

## Not sure which fits?

Start with what you do today. If you collect cash at the door, choose cash or bank transfer and carry on. You can move up when your club is ready.
